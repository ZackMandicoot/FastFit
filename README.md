# FastFit
## Authors: Zachary Tamana (edy7qm) and Mariam Adly (wmv7tn)
* **Purpose:** FastFit is designed to be a space where users can view healthy meal options from restaurants in Charlottesville. It allows users to save their favorite meals for easy access when unsure of what to eat and has the intention of helping individuals who are trying to be fit and healthy while having to eat out at fast food places.
